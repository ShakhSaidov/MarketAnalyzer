# app.py

A lightweight charting tool for exploring OHLC market data and supporting strategy backtesting. It provides an interactive way to inspect price action, replay historical market data, and switch between different timeframes.

## Features

- **OHLC Charting** — Load and visualize Open, High, Low, and Close market data.
- **Backtesting Tools** — Basic tools to help analyze price movements and review trading setups.
- **Replay Mode** — Replay historical market data step by step to examine how price action develops over time.
- **Multiple Timeframes** — Switch between different timeframes to analyze the market at various levels of detail.
- **Visual Market Analysis** — Explore historical price data and review potential trading opportunities directly on the chart.
